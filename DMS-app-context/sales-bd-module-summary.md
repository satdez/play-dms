# Sales / BD Module — Summary

BD covers the top-of-funnel: outreach, proposals, inbound requests, and a knowledge base of past clients for pitching new business — rolled up on a team dashboard.

## Dashboard
- Shows closed revenue, pipeline revenue, and conversion rate for a chosen date range.
- Tracks the outreach funnel (total / closed / pipeline) and proposal funnel (total / closed / pipeline).
- Lists recent outreach activity.

## Outreach
- Logs contacts made with prospects (Brand or Agency), with POC and source.
- New outreach is created against a brand and a point of contact, either existing or added on the fly.
- Each outreach can be moved forward into a **Proposal**, or **Disqualified**.

## Proposals
- Lists every proposal with status, source, service type(s), and owner.
- Statuses: Mapped To POC → Approved → Converted To Campaign, or Cancelled.
- Creating one captures a brief (with AI-assisted drafting), projected billing cost, pitch date, internal owner, and which services are offered (Amplification, Influencers, ORM, Advanced Campaign Analytics, Miscellaneous, SOLO, Twitter Trend, Content Creation, Offline Activity, Memed).
- A won proposal converts into an actual campaign.

## Inbound Requests
- Captures interest coming to WLDD directly (web or email), separate from BD's own outreach.
- Shows contact info, budget, source, and a summary (with the original email viewable for email-sourced requests).
- Each request can be rejected or turned into a tracked outreach, feeding the same Outreach → Proposal pipeline.

## Knowledge Base
- A searchable archive of past clients with industry and campaign count, sortable and filterable.
- Used as reference material to inform outreach and proposals — looking up a client's history before pitching.

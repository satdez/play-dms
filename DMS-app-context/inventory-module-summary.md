# Inventory Module — Summary

Inventory onboards creators/influencers as "pages," prices them, and approves them for use in campaign plans — through three levels of increasing privilege.

## Access Levels
- **Inventory Team (L1)** — onboards adhoc pages and new signups; sets internal cost only; can't see pitching cost.
- **Inventory Lead (L2)** — reviews Team's submissions, adds pitching cost, and approves within their assigned category/tag access.
- **Inventory Head (L3)** — full control, can overwrite any cost, gives final approval, and manages what each Lead can see via category-based "Manage Permissions" (current) or legacy "Tags" (deprecated but still functional).
- Leads and the Head can both review a request directly — Lead approval isn't a prerequisite for the Head to act on it.

## Page Onboarding
- New page signup → Team sets internal cost → Lead adds pitching cost → Head reviews and gives final approval → page becomes usable in campaign plans.
- **Adhoc pages** (added mid-campaign because they're not in inventory yet) follow the same three-step approval chain, but stay locked to the campaign they were added for until the Head fully approves them — only then can other campaigns use them too.

## Cost-Variable Pages
- When added to a plan, priced automatically at their last-approved internal cost.
- Pricing updates afterward go through the **Missing Costs** page rather than editing the plan directly; costs can also be reset back to needing a fresh entry.

## Other Features
- **Scouting Agents** — surfaces new pages to onboard directly from Instagram.
- **Content Tags** — buckets pages by content type for browsing/filtering.

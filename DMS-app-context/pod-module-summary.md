# POD Module 

POD is the core unit for running a campaign end-to-end: **Creation → Planning → Execution → Tracking → Reporting.**

## Creation
- Anyone assigned a pod role, pod, and cohort can create a campaign.
- The name is generated this way - "CLient Name" X "Activity"
- A dedicated set of Drive folders is provisioned automatically when the campaign starts, to store samples etc.
- Planner and tracker are switched on based on included services (Amplification, SOLO, Influencers, Meme'd).
- Every campaign moves through six stages: **Brief → Team Allocation → Pitch Details → Campaign Details → Activation → Campaign Report**, ending in Complete.

## Planner
- The team builds the distribution plan by adding inventory pages into categories.
- Each page carries deliverables (reel, post, story, etc.), with cost recorded at the time of adding.
- The plan can be exported to a Google Sheet to share with the client.

## Tracker
- This is used to track deliverables during campaign execution after plan is approved by client.
- Holds one row per deliverable.
- Links come in two ways: the team pastes them directly, or vendors submit via the vendor portal (once the team opts a campaign in for vendor visibility).
- Vendor-submitted links always need manual review, which also stops vendors from guessing whether something was already posted.
- Exports to a client-facing sheet, row by row.

## Reports
- The client-facing reports page has two parts:**gallery** of submissions and **reports**.
- Reports can run lightweight (post metrics only) or deep (also analyzes comments and surfaces topics), controlled by a per-campaign setting.

## Miscellaneous-Service Campaigns
- Services like ORM, Advanced Campaign Analytics, Offline Activity, and Twitter Trend skip the page-based planner entirely.
- Procurement assigns a vendor; the vendor submits work and raises an invoice.
- The invoice is approved first by the campaign lead, then processed by procurement.

## Knowledge base
- Place where you can browse clients and their campaign history.
